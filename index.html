01 / ABOUT ME!!!

About Me.

I come from China and am currently studying Digital Media at the University of Leeds. With a background in Arts and Crafts and Graphic Design, I am interested in visual culture and creative expression.

I founded STAR TRUCK, an independent design studio focusing on graphic design, 3D visual design, and album cover artwork. My work explores distinctive visual styles inspired by music and digital culture.
